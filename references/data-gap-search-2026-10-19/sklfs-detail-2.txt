It was introduced in Wildfire Smoke Detection System: Model Architecture, Training Mechanism, and Dataset to support systematic evaluation of wildfire smoke detection models under practical conditions. The test split contains 50,735 images from 3,309 videos, including 3,588 smoke-positive images from 340 real wildfire videos. The dataset also includes a large number of negative samples with smoke-like interference objects, making it suitable for evaluating model robustness in unpredictable open environments. Key Features Real-world early wildfire scenes collected from practical application data. 50,735 test images from 3,309 videos. 3,588 smoke-positive images from 340 real wildfire videos. Difficult negative samples with smoke-like visual interference. Bounding-box-level annotation for smoke detection evaluation.
